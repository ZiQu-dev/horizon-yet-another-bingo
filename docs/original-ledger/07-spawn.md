### Parity risks
Ordered by how visible they are to a player.
1. Lobby mode: original puts every player in ADVENTURE in the lobby and in pregame (spawn-15); port does not, so players can build and break in the lobby.
2. Start wait: original shows "Loading terrain..." with percent on the ready panel and hotbar (spawn-35, spawn-36); port shows nothing, and staging at y=150 in spectator mode is the only wait (spawn-33).
3. Team placement: different algorithm (vanilla spreadplayers at 0,0 with a (0,1000) range vs. the original's ring and biome/water rules); spawn distance 0 gives separate spawns, not one shared spawn; teammates stacked on the representative with no offset (spawn-02, -03, -04, -10).
4. Elytra: original gives an unbreakable elytra with 2 rockets and re-gives it every tick if lost; port gives one ordinary, breakable elytra, once, no rockets (spawn-22, -23).
5. Kit equipment: original equips armour into equipment slots and gives the kit again after death; port's /give leaves armour in inventory and gives nothing on death (spawn-27, -30).
6. Respawn/reset: effects, XP, food, held items, recipes, statistics and advancements are not reset on respawn in the port (spawn-40 to -43). Visible if a player dies in a round.
7. Post-game mode: original gives SPECTATOR (card HUD clients) or CREATIVE; port gives SURVIVAL (spawn-18).
8.
