### Parity risks
1. Whole visual language: the original panel is concrete blocks and light levels (white ON, gray OFF, red danger, lime title); the port's panel is translucent dark text rectangles. Most visible difference. Fix would need block displays (or a different reproduction) for tiles.
2. Panel layout: the original arranges 13 or so tiles across the wall in fixed columns (modes, goal, difficulty, teams, players, start), with icons; the port has an 11-tile text panel and a separate chest. Players see two different menus.
3. Reset Defaults runs at once in the port; the original asks first (ResetPrompt with a red confirm). Players can lose all rules by one click.
4. Start: the original tile is gray and sneak-click starts with spectators; the port's tile is white and the sneak path is not found.
5. Team pad: no dust particle, no join sound (SOUND_TEAM_CHANGED unused); pad name is at 1.4 not 1.5 and not VERTICAL-billboarded; the join box is a block, not a 2x4 box.
6. Reroll: no primed-TNT sound on each click and no TNT spam at 100 clicks (SOUND_REROLL unused); reroll die glyph missing.
7. Goal and item-distribution editors: the original has +/- steppers (goal 1..25/1..11, tier counts 0..25 with sum colouring and per-tier tooltips); the port has fixed choices and presets only. Players cannot set arbitrary goals in the menu.
8. Modes: the original's four game-mode flags (lockout, inventory, hidden items, consume items) are independent toggles; the port's Standard/Inventory/Lockout is one exclusive choice. Behaviour, not only look: a player cannot combine lockout and inventory in the port's menu.
9. Preset list: the original's panel shows four presets (no impossible); the chest shows five. Minor; say which one is intended.
10. Live info: "Players: n / max" and the best-time text are live in the original; the port's Players tile is a static title, and there is no best time in the menu.
11. Tooltips: the original shows a hover tooltip for every tile (titles in green, description, and for presets the five tier counts coloured); the port has none on the panel (chest lore only).
12. Filters: objective filter radio (ItemFilters.kt) is not reachable from the port's menu.
13. Warnings: the original shows a yellow body on a dark backdrop with a red danger button, and any severe GameWarningEvent switches to it; the port shows a bell item after a refused start.
14. Missing toggles: Preview card and Unlock recipes (Features page) do not exist in the port's menu.
15. Panel orientation and placement: text facing is a guessed rotation (PANEL_ROTATION comment); needs live check (menu-a-23).
