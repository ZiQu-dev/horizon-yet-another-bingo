### Parity risks

1. Lobby stats panel is missing entirely (stats-23 to stats-27). In the original, a player stepping on a heavy weighted pressure plate in the lobby sees their stats on the armour stand panel (name, skull, two text columns). This is the most visible gap in this slice.
2. Chat stats text is plain: no star or calendar glyph, no bold or colour, and a colon after the titles that the original does not have (stats-05 to stats-07). Players comparing the two will see it at once.
3. Reset commands are absent (stats-28). `reset player`, `reset game`, `reset all` have no port command, and their en_us keys have no caller. Operators who expect to clear high scores cannot.
4. Favorite team is absent (stats-27). Only the lobby panel shows it, so it goes with risk 1 but is still a missing feature in the data layer.
5. Singleplayer best time may be wrong (stats-16, suspected). In the original the V4 migration makes `BestTime`'s primary key (bingo_options_hash, host_id, is_singleplayer), so singleplayer and multiplayer rounds are separate rows. The singleplayer query reads only singleplayer rows. The port's `best_time` (stats.rs:86-96) instead returns None when the first round of the rules had more than one player, and otherwise takes the minimum over all rounds, multiplayer included. Its test (dev/tests/server_stats.rs:150-162) encodes a "one-row table" model that the V4 schema does not have. Verify against the SQL before relying on the single-player best time.
6. Sync is not implemented, but channels.rs:61-63 marks stats_check, stats_index and stats_game as Done. Only the codecs are done. A player who joins with a stats-capable client gets no hash, and no game history moves between servers (stats-30 to stats-34). The status text should be corrected to avoid a false "done".
7. Monthly block boundaries (stats-11): the original uses the stored wall-clock start time. The port computes start as end minus in-game duration. Rounds with pauses, or ended near midnight on the last day of the month, can land in a different month.
8. Captured items per card (stats-19): the original counts achieved objectives on each completed card; the port's ledger is keyed by team and square index, not by card. When a team plays several cards in one round (playto cards), a square index on the next card can overwrite or clear the finder recorded for the previous card. Affects the best-captured-items end-screen line and the `items` column of `stats`. Check with a two-card round.
9. Best captured items are shown on the end screen and compared with the best from earlier rounds of the same rules (stats-18). The rules key differs (FNV-1a in the port, SHA-512 in the original, stats-41). This does not show to players, but a stats database copied from the original would not match.
10. Storage (stats-35, stats-40): no import of an existing `bingo.db`, and no host id. Players moving from the original lose their history unless a migration is written. Server-wide best times and streaks in the port cover only the local history file.
11. Command name (stats-01): players type `/bingo stats` in the port and `/bingostats` in the original.
