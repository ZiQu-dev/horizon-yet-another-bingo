### Parity risks
Ordered by how visible they are to a player.
1. Lobby never starts by itself. Original counts down 10:00 from the first team pick (startWhenReadyWaitsForFirstVote=false). Port needs one /ready press (server/ready.rs:133). Players see no countdown until someone readies. (ready-team-reset-03)
2. Returning to the lobby keeps teams in port; original clears all teams and spectators (keepTeamsAfterRoundEnds=false). Players stay on teams in port without rejoining. (ready-team-reset-49)
3. Postgame: port has no countdown, no vote buttons (server never sets is_ready_vote_available), no default option, and Next Round goes to the lobby instead of starting the next round. (ready-team-reset-23/24/25/26)
4. Team names in chat: original "[Red Team]" coloured with member hover; port "Red", plain. (ready-team-reset-38)
5. Shuffle: original picks random teams and deals in chunks (4/2/2 for 8 players on 3 teams); port fixes teams to presets 1..n and round-robins (3/3/2). Port also refuses more than 8 teams. (ready-team-reset-42, -43, -44)
6. Joining: port `join spectators` is an error; already-on-team and unknown-team texts differ; joiner gets no "You have been moved" line; mid-game joining is open to everyone. (ready-team-reset-32..36, 47)
7. Ready feedback: "Cannot ready up - other players need to join a team!" missing; ready-up keybind hint ("Ready up! [Sneak] + [Open card]") missing; the "not on a team" hint names "/spectator" (should be "/join spectators"). (ready-team-reset-08, -09, -16)
8. Countdown denominator uses all online players in port (remaining_ms, server/ready.rs:219-224) vs eligible players in original; changing it changes how fast readies shorten the timer. (ready-team-reset-04)
9. Ready clears on any team switch in port; original keeps it. Ready is also cleared on disconnect in port. (ready-team-reset-10, -58)
10. Reset: port resets after 5 s (100 ticks) vs original 1 s; no world template or fixed-seed config; card seed is bumped by 1, not preserved by options. (ready-team-reset-62..64)
11. Packet cadence and fallbacks: packets every 10 ticks vs every tick; no V1 send; no action-bar fallback for clients without the mod. (ready-team-reset-18..20)
12. Options change does not restart the ready timer in port. (ready-team-reset-59)
