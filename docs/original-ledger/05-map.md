### Parity risks
Ordered by how visible they are to a player.
1. Map item names and lore differ from the original: the port hard-codes "Card preview", "Team card (<name>)" and "Team card (<name>, won|lost)" in Rust (hb/server/map_items.rs item_snbt, L127-141), where the original shows "Preview Card", "Bingo Card (<name>)", "Trophy of the Bingo Victor" and "Relic of a Lost Bingo". The memento has no lore at all (map-22). This also breaks the translation rule in CLAUDE.md.
2. No card map items reach players at all. bingo-native/src/server.rs new_map returns None (PORT-GAP, L480-483), so slot_for fails and give() never gives an item. For a client without the card HUD, the held card, the preview, spectator cards and mementos are all absent (map-24, map-27). Right-click to open the card and the game-over dialog path are missing with it (map-27, map-28).
3. Card open message: when nothing is viewable, the original shows the hotbar line "There are no Bingo Cards to display..."; the port's client always opens the Cards tab (map-29, map-30).
4. Map refresh lag: the original repaints on the change tick; the port waits up to 10 ticks (map-02). Scoring flashes on maps look slightly late.
5. Custom tile images are not drawn on the map (map-43), and the card image cap (16 images, 512 px, 1 MiB) is a port-only limit (map-16).
6. Preview card ignores the showPreviewCard option and lobby-mode check (map-17). Memento ignores lobby mode and giveMementoInSurvival (map-23).
7. Map border and tile drawing geometry was checked for constants only (map-04); a line-by-line diff of drawMapBorders, apply_decorations (count digits, frames, plus mark) and the colour-distance rule (map-06, map-12, map-13) is still needed.
8. Item tags: the original items carry IGNORE, VANISH and KEEP tags (so they survive death and drops); the port sets only bingo_card (map-45).
