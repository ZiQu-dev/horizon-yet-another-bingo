### Parity risks
Ordered by how visible to a player:
1. Advancement toasts, advancement chat and death chat in the lobby and PREGAME (mixins-impl-11, -13): the original hides them and blocks awards for non-team players; the port has no gate.
2. Recipe book contents (mixins-impl-15): the original unlocks or locks all recipes per option on spawn, silently; the port never touches recipes.
3. Death and drop behaviour (mixins-impl-08, -09): the info book vanishes on drop and is kept on death in the original; the port writes the tags but nothing reads them. A player who dies with the book loses it in the port.
4. Dialog screens (mixins-impl-71): game over, lobby mode choice, lobby confirmation and map item preview are dialogs in the original; the port has none.
5. Tab list check mark for ready players (mixins-impl-10): missing in the port; the ready state is only on the HUD.
6. Team start loading wait (mixins-impl-14): the original starts the countdown only after every playing player has moved (40 ms after loading, 40 s cap); the port has no such wait.
7. Respawn and End exit points (mixins-impl-01..04): team spawn on respawn and the overworld for End exits are missing; a bed-less player respawns at the world spawn.
8. Hunger in the lobby (mixins-impl-23): food is not held at 10 or reset on spawn in the port.
9. Time of day and tick freeze (mixins-impl-43, -44): the original resets time to 0 at countdown and start and freezes ticks; the port freezes by effects and never sets time.
10. Lobby sounds and map pictures (mixins-impl-06, -26): sound mute option and map item pictures are not in the port (PORT-GAP lib.rs:395 and server.rs:480 for maps).
11. Team pad particles and interactions (mixins-impl-24, -25): the original's pads take left and right clicks and show dust particles; the port takes only right clicks on blocks and shows no dust.
12. Spectator mode (mixins-impl-36, -39): the original keeps non-team players in adventure with read-only chest views; the port puts them in spectator mode.
13. Menu blur and fades (mixins-impl-27, -28): blur is always full and item/text fades are not drawn (PORT-GAP).
14. Gamerules in the lobby (mixins-impl-12): random ticks and fire spread are not zeroed in the lobby editor.
15. Title and subtitle messages (mixins-impl-41): the port uses the action bar.
