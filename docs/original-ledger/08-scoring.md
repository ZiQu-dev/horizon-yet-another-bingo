### Parity risks
1. No message feed and no time prefix: the server sends plain chat lines, never game_message packets, so the Horizon message list is empty and every chat line lacks `[hh:mm:ss]` (scoring-16, scoring-17). Most visible of all the scoring gaps.
2. Card completed effects are never sent: no fireworks and no GameWon/GameLost sound for the team that completes a card (scoring-08). This is the likely "game-over effect" the player noticed.
3. Lost items are silent: no shulker sound and no "lost" line (scoring-04, scoring-05).
4. Hidden items mode leaks names: the port prints the real item name to all teams in chat (scoring-02).
5. Wording and colour of chat: completed-card text is different and has no stalemate variant; no yellow, aqua, red or gray formatting exists in texts::Line (server.rs:866 sends plain text) (scoring-06, 07, 09, 10, 11).
6. Missing wait line: `You've finished the game! Wait...` is replaced by `%s reached the goal!` for all players (scoring-10).
7. End-screen ranking: ties do not share a place, and every team gets the game's duration (scoring-23, scoring-24). Visible on the end screen.
8. Score sounds: the port's sound keys and pitch formula differ from the vanilla fallback; confirm against the mod's client receiver before changing (scoring-19).
9. Options showCompletedItems, showCompletedLines and showLeadingTeam are constants (session.rs:214-216), so a server that turns them off behaves differently (scoring-03, scoring-18).
10. Team-change removal of scored items is not found (scoring-31); stalemate and replace-goal paths are not fully read (scoring-25, scoring-27).
